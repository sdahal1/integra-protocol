Integra	Protocol	Basic	Method	   1. When you think about (experience, problem, goal), how do you feel? (X) (Record)  2. Feel X and tell me, is there anything bad about it? (Until "No")  3. Feel X and tell me, is there anything good about it? (Until "No")  4. Feel X... and now feel yourself... feel your identity... what identity is that, what kind of person? (person A) (Record)  5. Feel person A. From the point of view of person A, what is your need? (Intention, Goal) (Until "No")  6. Feel person A. From the point of view of person A, what is your belief? (Conclusion, Decision) (Until "No")  7. If this identity (person A) vanished, could something bad happen? (Until "No")  8. If this identity (person A) vanished, could something good happen? (Until "No")  -Check the result: When you think about (experience, problem, goal), how do you feel?
