Integra	Protocol	Standard	Method	1. When you think about (experience, problem, goal), how do you feel? (X) (Record)  2. Feel X and tell me, is there something bad about it? (Until "No") 3. Feel X and tell me, is there something good about it? (Until "No")  4. Feel X... and now feel yourself... feel your identity... what identity is that, what kind of person? (person A) (Record)  5. Feel person A. From the point of view of person A, what is your need? (Intention, Goal) (Until "No") 6. Feel person A. From the point of view of person A, what is your belief? (Conclusion, Decision) (Until "No")  7. Instead of person A, what kind of person would you like to be? (person B) (Record)  8. Feel person B. From the point of view of person B, what is your need? (Intention, Goal) (Until "No") 9. Feel person B. From the point of view of person B, what is your belief? (Conclusion, Decision) (Until "No")  10. Feel both person A and person B at the same time, and as you feel them at the same time, inhale and exhale.  11. Can you feel them separately? (If no, check the result. If yes, continue.)  12. If person A and person B merged, could something bad happen? (Until "No") 13. If person A and person B merged, could something good happen? (Until "No")   14. Feel both person A and person B at the same time, and as you feel them at the same time, inhale and exhale.  15. Can you feel them separately? (If no, check the result. If yes, continue.)  16. Feel person A. Is A still the best name for that identity or can you give me a better one? (Record if new)	(Return to number 5)  -Check the result: When you think about (experience, problem, goal), how do you feel?  -Additional question: Is there anything else regarding (experience, problem, goal)?
